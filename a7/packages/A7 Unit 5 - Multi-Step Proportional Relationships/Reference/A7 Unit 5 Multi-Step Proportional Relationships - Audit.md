## 7. Judgment calls

1. **Decision 1 names the wrong "FAST" numbers** — the sheet prints 1.61, 2.204, 0.454, 0.39, 0.62, not 1.609 or 2.2. Fix 5.06–07 board 3 (→ 8.05), 5.08–09 board 1 (→ 22.04), 5.10–11 Note II (→ 62 mi/h). The book's lessons drift; its assessment doesn't.
2. fl oz ↔ mL and 1 L = 1,000 cm³ are not on the sheet: route fl oz via 8 fl oz = 1 c and 0.24 L/c; print "1 mL = 1 cm³" on board 5 or cut it.
3. Add a part-to-whole mixture (5.10–11 board 7) and a two-hop currency chain (5.06–07 board 7) — both are the state's own shapes.
4. Add an "is it a scale copy?" board to 5.01 and one triangle to 5.02–03; rectangles alone under-teach GR.1.5.
5. Name ounce vs fluid ounce in 5.08–09 vocabulary.
6. Weighting: the book gives AR.3.3 48 %, ours 33 %. Keep ours (three of six days are GR.1.5); both transfer items carry a conversion anyway.
7. Keep angles-don't-scale, the MC diagnostics and estimate-first; the book has none, and IXL WEA asks for the angle.
8. Mathlandia stays out; 5.6's dated real rates are the model (date it, print it, never ask for it).
