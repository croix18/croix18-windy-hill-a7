## 6.01  Triangle Inequality Theorem; Converse of the Pythagorean Theorem

*MA.8.GR.1.3*

**Variation.**  Boards 1–2: a triangle? (no, then yes with two equal sides); 3 votes with the equal case and the wrong pair; 4–5 the third side's range, written then voted. The pattern breaks at 6 — a RIGHT triangle?, squares not sums — and 7 lists the longest side first; 8 has the extra number; 9 is written. The independent set walks the same path.

**Read this once, when you plan the unit.**

- Two book lessons in one day (6.1 + 6.2, the plan's own merge: 6.2 is 6.1's practice). Built from the benchmark and the B1G-M first, then read against Math Nation (the unit audit).
- The Pythagorean Theorem itself is taught on Notes III as the fact the converse turns around — the 3-4-5 squares — and is not yet used to find a side (6.03). The warm-up's Q3–Q4 (√169, 5² + 12²) preload the first converse before it has a name.
- Every converse board names its sides in an order that is not 'leg, leg, hypotenuse' at least once (Example 2, board 7, the independent set's Q5, bank Q7): the test is on the LONGEST side wherever it is listed — the Source of Truth's named misconception for GR.1.1, misidentifying the hypotenuse.
- The equal case (4 + 5 = 9) is the triangle inequality's whole trap and the Florida reference sheet's '>' is strict: Notes I draws it lying flat, and boards 3 and 5 make it a choice to refuse.

**What the audit against Math Nation found.**

- Every item re-derived by sympy at build time (mathcheck: 0 findings); every 'no' is checked as strictly less or equal, every 'right' as an exact equality of squares.
- Math Nation 6.1–6.2 read against this lesson (A7 Unit 6 Audit): its item shapes worth keeping are here with our own numbers; none of its numbers is reused on a board.

**What changed from the book.**

- 6.1 and 6.2 openers (exploration with paper strips, in pairs) → Notes I, taught from the front with three sets of sticks, under the no-partner-work rule.

- 6.2 (the third side's range) → Notes II and boards 4–5.
- 6.1 and 6.2 Wrap-Ups → the independent set and IXL under rulings 10 and 21; board 9 is the exit evidence.
