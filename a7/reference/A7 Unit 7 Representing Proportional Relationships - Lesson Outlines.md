## The unit's standing decisions (each reversible)

1. **Where the class stands.** Unit 11 (December) graphed lines from a table of values — x = 0 and two more, the
   third point the check — and taught "the coefficient of x". The class has no word "slope": Unit 10 names it on
   19 Jan. So **"slope" and "y-intercept" are on no student page**; a steeper line is "more y for each 1 of x", and
   a line that misses the origin has "a number added".
2. **One definition, said the same way every day:** *two quantities are in a proportional relationship when one is
   always the same number times the other: y = px.* That number, p, is the **constant of proportionality** (named
   in 7.02+03; on 7.01 it is the unit rate, a grade 6 word). Florida writes p; "some books write k" is said once, in 7.05+06.
3. **Three tests, in these words:** *table* — y ÷ x is the same number in **every** row; *graph* — a straight line
   **and** through the origin (0, 0); *description* — a steady rate with nothing added at the start. From 7.04:
   *equation* — y = px with nothing added.
4. **Which quantity is y is said in every item** (the state's first misconception is the ratio taken in the wrong
   order): a table names its rows, a graph its axes, a description ends with its two quantities. A constant carries
   its unit as "y-unit per x-unit": dollars per pound. Either quantity may be the subject (m = 60h, or h = (1/60)m):
   taught in 7.05+06 and asked on the test with the direction named.
5. **Negative constants** are in the benchmark's boundary (AR.4.2): taught in 7.02+03 in a mathematical context
   (a table, and a line falling through the origin), one question on the test.
6. **Graphs have readable points** (the line crosses grid corners), and **an axis scale is not always 1**: "read
   the scales first" is said from 7.01, and every lesson has at least one graph scaled by 2, 4, 5, 10 or 20.
   Where a choice question shows several graphs, all of them are on the same axes and scales. A context is drawn in
   Quadrant I; a bare y = px may run through Quadrant III.
7. **Whiteboards read; the Independent Set draws** (Unit 11's decision). Boards show a graph or a table and ask
   for a value, a point, a constant, an equation, a reason. The six-question handout prints empty grids on 7.01;
   **Your Turn 1 of 7.01 is the handout's question 1, drawn in class with the teacher circulating**, as 11.07 did.
8. **MA.8.AR.3.1 lives in 7.04 and in one test question:** a linear relationship is also proportional only when
   its line passes through the origin — Unit 11's y = 2x + 3 against y = 2x.
9. **No cross products** (the state: they "may lead to errors"). Ratios are tested with y ÷ x.
10. **Letters** fit the quantities (c = 7.5n) and never clash with p, the constant; never o, i or l. Multiplication
    is a dot or parentheses, never ×. Money is written with two decimals; a constant may be a fraction or a
    decimal, and both are accepted where both are exact.
11. **Calculators are allowed and never mentioned** (ruling of 2 Sep).
