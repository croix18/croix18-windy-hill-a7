# Scope & Sequence — the county's 2026–2027 sheets

> **This is not what either course is running this year.**
>
> The county issued these for 2026–2027, but built them around the textbook being adopted **next**
> year. Run against Math Nation they jump Unit 3 → 8 → 9 → 10 → 7 → 11 → 4 → 2 across the year,
> and since the second book does not reach students until the start of Q3, following them would
> mean seventh graders carrying two textbooks and switching between them constantly.
>
> **For this final Math Nation year, both courses run the old sequence — the book's own order.**
> On-level skips Math Nation Unit 1 and runs sequentially from Unit 2. Accelerated runs its own
> book from Unit 1.
>
> These sheets are transcribed and kept because they are **next year's map**, and because the
> benchmark-to-topic pairings in them are still the county's current thinking about what is
> essential. Use them for that. Do not use them to pace this year.

---

Transcribed from the district *Scope & Sequence Guidance* sheets for **M/J Grade 7 Mathematics**
and **M/J Grade 7 Accelerated Mathematics**. Essential benchmarks are the ones LCS teacher teams
identified; supporting benchmarks are what the essential one rests on. "Unit / Lesson" refers to
**Math Nation**, and the unit numbering is **per course** — Unit 8 in the accelerated book is not
Unit 8 in the on-level book.

### ⚠ Two errors in the district sheet

1. **Linear Functions / Slope-Intercept Form** — the unit line reads *Unit 10 Lessons 4 & 7–9*
   but the lesson list ends **13.9**. Every other lesson in that row is Unit 10, so this is
   almost certainly **10.9** mistyped. Worth confirming before anyone plans off it.
2. **Linear Inequalities in One Variable** — the unit line reads *Unit 1 Lessons 8–9* but the
   lesson list gives **three** lessons, 1.8, 1.9 and 1.10. Either the range or the list is wrong.

## Where the two courses meet

Three benchmarks are essential in **both** courses, so item banks and figures can be shared even
though the Math Nation lessons differ:

| Benchmark | M7 | A7 |
|---|---|---|
| MA.7.AR.2.2 two-step equations | Q2 · Unit 10 L5–10 | Q1 · Unit 1 L3–7 |
| MA.7.AR.4.3 proportional relationships | Q2 · Unit 11 L6–8 | Q2 · Unit 7 L1, 2, 7 |
| MA.7.GR.1.4 circles | Q3 · Unit 4 L8–10, Unit 11 L9 | Q3 · Unit 8 L4–7 |

Order of operations is essential in both but under **different benchmarks** — MA.7.NSO.2.1 for
on-level, MA.8.NSO.1.7 for accelerated, which adds radicals. Not shareable as-is.
