## What the order tells us (for the UNIT AUDIT checks)
- Eight of the 40 Grade 8 benchmarks land in Units 14–17, i.e. Q4 by the book's own pacing:
  AR.1.1–1.3, GR.1.4–1.6, DP.2.1–2.3, plus GR.2.x in 15. Nothing in the FAST blueprint waits for
  the calendar; PM2 (mid-year) will test them cold. Worth a decision on pulling Unit 14 forward
  next to Unit 3 (same laws, algebraic bases) and Unit 16 next to Unit 6 (both triangles).
- Unit 6 (Pythagorean, similar triangles) runs BEFORE Unit 7 (proportional relationships, MA.7).
  6.7–6.8 use proportional reasoning the course formalizes in Unit 7 — grade 6 ratio reasoning
  covers it, but it is a "technique before the tool" finding for the audit (soft, like M7 U4 L05).
- Unit 10 opens with 10.1 Proportional Relationships (8.AR.3.1) and Unit 12 closes with 12.8–12.9
  (8.F.1.2) — the "Also Assesses" pair AR.3.1 → F.1.2 is split across two units; plan a revisit.
- 4.5–4.8 (NSO.1.7, radicals in order of operations) come one unit after Unit 2 (roots) and right
  after Unit 3 (exponents) — the dependency chain is intact.
- IXL has no skill for 13.4, 16.4, 9.3, 9.5, 8.4, 8.7, 7.3, 7.6, 10.8, 5.5, 5.6 — those days need
  our own bank for the last five minutes, or a neighbouring lesson's skill.
- IXL's grade-8 "Solve multi-step inequalities" is attached to A7 lessons 1.9–1.10, which sit on
  8.AR.2.2 (two-step only). The IXL skill exceeds the benchmark; it is fine as practice, but the
  capcheck cap stays two-step for our own items.
